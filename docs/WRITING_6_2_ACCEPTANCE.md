# Writing 6.2 Acceptance

## Production Changes

- Whole-batch topic commissioning precedes grouped detailed briefs. Each assignment carries its own sector, decision, evidence angle and title intent.
- Historical content is passed as used titles and questions, not full customer narratives that encourage imitation.
- Editorial instructions distinguish company self-description from verified external evidence. Company names alone do not establish competitor strengths or weaknesses.
- Article bodies use Markdown transport, preserving model prose without long JSON-string escaping. Topic briefs remain structured JSON.
- Brand images are placed at opening/closing boundaries, not at arbitrary paragraph percentages.
- Versioned deployment waits until running production jobs finish, retains the previous service configuration, and does not replace project data.
